# Accelerated brain CEST MRI

**Status:** Peer-reviewed article in Magnetic Resonance in Medicine, 2025; published online in 2024. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.30269).

CEST MRI samples a spectrum of frequency offsets. Acquiring fewer offsets can reduce scan time, but quantitative maps must remain useful.

Our study combined Fisher-information-guided offset selection with U-Net estimation. I am the first author of the paper, which included data from 18 volunteers.

## Reported evidence

In the prospective experiment, acquisition used 15 of 53 offsets and achieved a 3.5-fold scan-time reduction. Reported agreement with reference maps included SSIM of 0.84 and Pearson correlation of 0.82.

The study also evaluated simulated pathology. That experiment is distinct from validation in patients. The acceleration figure describes acquisition time, not a claim about software inference speed or diagnostic accuracy.

## Skills demonstrated

Experimental design, sampling optimization, neural-network estimation, quantitative validation and translation from retrospective analysis to prospective measurement.
