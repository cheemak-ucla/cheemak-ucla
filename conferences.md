# Conference presentations and abstracts

Selected work includes two oral presentations at SCMR, four first-author digital posters at ISMRM, and four abstracts submitted to ISMRM 2027. Submitted abstracts are listed separately from completed presentations.

## Oral presentations

### Free-breathing Ungated Cardiac Creatine Steady-State CEST Imaging at 3T

**SCMR 2025 · Washington, DC · Oral presentation · First author and presenter**

Presented January 31, 2025, in *Science Session 5: Oxygenation and Metabolic Imaging with CMR*. The work explores free-breathing, ungated cardiac creatine imaging with steady-state CEST at 3T.

[Published conference abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocmr.2024.101336) · [Conference program](https://2025scmr.eventscribe.net/fsPopup.asp?PresentationID=1546958&efp=WFJVUkFXWU8yMzYzNg&mode=presInfo)

*Journal of Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance.* 2025;27(Suppl 1):101336. This publication is a conference abstract.

### Creatine assessment in acute myocardial infarction using CEST

**SCMR–ISMRM Workshop at SCMR 2023 · San Diego, CA · Oral presentation · First author and presenter**

Presented in the workshop session *MRS, MT, CEST*. The work investigates CEST-based assessment of creatine in acute myocardial infarction.

[Public dissertation record](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/93b6j7jz) · [Dissertation PDF, selected oral presentations on page xvii](https://escholarship.org/content/qt93b6j7jz/qt93b6j7jz_noSplash_fe0ab6d5584e6281fa8bcce10e8651a1.pdf)

## ISMRM digital posters

### Obtaining exchange rate maps using Multitasking SS-CEST

**ISMRM 2024 · Singapore · Digital poster · First author · Abstract 4463**

Presented May 9, 2024. The study evaluates accelerated three-dimensional quantitative CEST acquisition and exchange-rate mapping in a porcine spine model.

[Abstract](https://archive.ismrm.org/2024/4463.html) · [Session program](https://www.ismrm.org/24/pf/D-68.htm)

### Predicting Pain caused by the Intervertebral Disc Degeneration in Large Animal Model Using CEST MRI Data

**ISMRM 2024 · Singapore · Digital poster · First author · Abstract 4117**

Presented May 8, 2024. The study examines relationships between CEST-derived imaging measurements and pain scores after intervertebral disc injury in a porcine model.

[Abstract](https://archive.ismrm.org/2024/4117_XrAVtNSdx.html) · [Session program](https://www.ismrm.org/24/pf/D-183.htm)

### CEST Mapping from Undersampled Z-spectra in the Brain Using Deep Learning

**ISMRM 2023 · Toronto, Canada · Digital poster · First author · Abstract 3164**

Presented June 6, 2023. The study combines Fisher-information-guided frequency selection and deep learning to estimate CEST maps from undersampled measurements.

[Abstract](https://archive.ismrm.org/2023/3164.html) · [Session program](https://www.ismrm.org/23/program-files/D-86.htm)

### Amide Proton Transfer (APT) Mapping from Undersampled Z-spectra in the Brain Using Deep Learning

**ISMRM 2022 · London, UK · Digital poster · First author · Abstract 2524**

Presented May 12, 2022. The study explores deep-learning estimation of APT maps from fewer CEST frequency offsets selected using Fisher information.

[Abstract](https://archive.ismrm.org/2022/2524.html) · [Session program](https://www.ismrm.org/22/program-files/D-33.htm)
